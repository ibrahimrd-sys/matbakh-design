The functional loss

A video cannot be recoloured by state

asset-spec.md requires stroke="currentColor" precisely so the reader can tint a glyph by what is happening. This is one file, four meanings.

normal
done
timer running
never

Four states, one 235-byte file. A clip would need four encodes, or a colour overlay that fights the photograph underneath. This is the strongest argument for glyphs staying in the tile even after a clip library exists.

The vocabulary

58% of the lexicon is motion. The rest should stay still.

Counted from content/lexicon/activities.yaml — 81 activities, 78 distinct glyphs. The classification is judgement, not measurement; contest it during the pilot.

Class M · motion-defined 47

A single frame cannot separate it from at least one neighbour. The board is 10 for 10; the stove is 15 of 19, because boil, simmer and poach are defined by how the liquid moves.

Class S · state-defined 34

A placement, a configuration, or an elapsed condition. A glyph is not a compromise here — it is correct. A four-second loop of rest is four seconds of nothing happening, at a cost.

The proposal for motion in cook mode: loop once on page turn, then hold the final frame; tap to replay. The cook gets the motion at the moment they arrive, the page then goes still, and the held frame is a legible picture. Four tiles looping forever is four competing motions in the periphery of someone holding a knife — philosophy §3 principle 5, the cook's place is sacred. On this page everything loops continuously, so you can judge the motion itself.

Standing caveats
  • The static and animated glyphs are the real design/icons/chop.svg and its neighbours, unmodified — which are themselves geometric placeholders, not the production Tabler set. C-05 is open.
  • The video and photograph columns are coloured rectangles. No bolognese has been shot. Nothing here is art direction.
  • The 47 / 34 split is counted from activities.yaml; the classification is judgement and the pilot should correct it.
  • The costs come from design/step-imagery-decision.md, where every derived figure is marked as derived.
  • Sizes are true only at 100% browser zoom. Pinch-zooming this page to inspect the 17 px glyphs defeats the point of them.
